How I got here.

Not a straight line. More a series of things I got curious about, and then got good at.

01

A creative foundation

I started on the creative side, and it's never really left. I notice when something is slightly off: the spacing, the message, the order things happen in. That instinct for how things look and feel still shapes how I approach almost everything.

Brands, teams and businesses

The bigger picture

Over time I got more interested in the questions around the work: what a business is really trying to say, who it needs to reach, and why. Communication, technology and commercial strategy sit much closer together than most org charts suggest.
